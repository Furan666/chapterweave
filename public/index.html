on the shelf.

Give each group one or two films, artists, or books they love. Add three to six books your club could actually read. ChapterWeave looks for a title that ranks near the top for both groups.

The method

  1. 01Start with people. Pick cultural titles that represent each group.
  2. 02Set the shelf. Use three to six books you can source for the club.
  3. 03Compare positions. A bridge must land in the top half for both groups.

Ranks are relative to each group’s own result list. For odd shelves, the top-half cutoff rounds down. Ranks do not predict attendance or confirm local availability.
